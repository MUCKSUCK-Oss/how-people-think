### 1a. "How Much Does It Cost To Promote a Crypto Scam?" (Dec 2021, ~9.5 min) [watched: all, 0:00–9:30]
**What it claims**
- Soulja Boy accidentally showed he was paid about $24,000 to promote a coin ([0:00](https://youtu.be/ryEQhaRQ6V4?t=0)).
- An NFT Instagram account asked $20–25k for a post that did not say it was an ad ([1:02](https://youtu.be/ryEQhaRQ6V4?t=62)).
- BitBoy's media kit: $5k for a review, $20k for a livestream, $40k for an interview. BitBoy confirmed the prices and said he always discloses ([2:07](https://youtu.be/ryEQhaRQ6V4?t=127)).

- The lesson: many people talking about getting rich are getting rich from *promoting* ([8:37](https://youtu.be/ryEQhaRQ6V4?t=517)).

**Fact-check**
- ✅ Soulja Boy's $24k: he tweeted it, then deleted it; the coin was a SafeMoon copy that later collapsed ([Protos](https://protos.com/soulja-boy-safermars-deleted-tweet-oopsie-celeb-touting-violations); [Benzinga](https://benzinga.com/markets/cryptocurrency/21/05/21337044/soulja-boy-accidentally-reveals-how-much-he-got-paid-to-promote-crypto-project-1)). In 2023 the US SEC charged several celebrities for crypto promotion without disclosing pay ([WealthManagement](https://www.wealthmanagement.com/regulation-compliance/sec-charges-celebs-touting-crypto-without-disclosing-compensation)).
- ❓ The individual price lists: leaked marketing documents. Coffeezilla says himself that he can't verify every name. Fair.

**Techniques he uses:** humour and sarcasm, a "secret list revealed" frame, a behind-the-curtain feeling. But he hedges honestly ("even if 50% is correct").

**What it claims**

- Logan Paul sold CryptoZoo as a fun game "that makes you money" ([3:09](https://youtu.be/386p68_lDHA?t=189)).

**Fact-check**
- ✅ The project failed, and a class action was filed in Feb 2023. In Jan 2024 Paul offered to buy back eggs and animals for 0.1 ETH each (about $2.3M in total), but only if buyers agreed not to sue. The ZOO token was left out ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/01/04/logan-paul-cryptozoo-nft-buy-back-class-action-lawsuit); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/211776/logan-paul-launches-cryptozoo-nft-buyback-files-countersuit-as-legal-battles-rage-on/)).
- ⚠️ ZOO token value: Paul himself said it fell from about $130M to $26M. I could not confirm the "~$2 billion" figure Coffeezilla mentions; that may be a fully diluted number. The token later lost over 99%.
- Paul sued Coffeezilla for defamation (2024). Paul and his team deny any "rug pull" and say they never sold their own NFTs.

**Techniques:** a victim montage as the emotional hook (identifiable victims), a detective "evidence board", a recorded phone confrontation, and a clear villain. These are strong persuasion tools even when the facts are right.

### 1c. "I Joined a Pump and Dump Scheme So You Don't Have To" (29 Apr 2021, 9:46) [watched: all]
**What it claims**
- Telegram and Discord "pump" groups (Galactic, Voyager, Big Pump Signal) pump a coin every 3–5 days, and groups coordinate ([2:02](https://youtu.be/ehDvr5vVGPg?t=122)).
- The "fair bot" that picks a random coin is a lie ([3:04](https://youtu.be/ehDvr5vVGPg?t=184)). A pump lasts about 5 minutes ([4:08](https://youtu.be/ehDvr5vVGPg?t=248)).
- Members are told outsiders will lose, but the trading record shows insiders buying slowly for weeks before. The members are the real losers ([6:14](https://youtu.be/ehDvr5vVGPg?t=374)).
- One group has 171k members, about 450k across platforms ([7:16](https://youtu.be/ehDvr5vVGPg?t=436)), and the groups prey on FOMO ([8:19](https://youtu.be/ehDvr5vVGPg?t=499)).

**Fact-check**
- ✅ Matches the academic study by Xu & Livshits (Imperial College), which looked at hundreds of Telegram pumps (2018–19): about 100 channels ran roughly 2 pumps a day. Organisers profit from insider knowledge "at the sacrifice of fellow pumpers"; only buyers in the first ~20 seconds can profit ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.10109); [Columbia blog](https://clsbluesky.law.columbia.edu/2019/01/07/cryptocurrency-pump-and-dump-schemes/)).

**Techniques:** "I went undercover so you don't have to", contempt for victims who were trying to cheat others, one clear twist ("you were the outsider all along"). **For the paper (6.9):** a perfect case of a *false insider feeling*. Belonging to the group makes people feel safe.

## 3. Patrick Boyle (finance tricks): supports 6.9 FOMO and 6.10 corporations

### 3a. "Robinhood is Being Investigated for Fraud by the SEC. Here's Why." (4 Sep 2020, 8:57) [watched: all]
**What it claims**
- Robinhood's main income is "payment for order flow" (PFOF): it is paid to send customers' orders to high-speed traders. This was about 70% of its revenue ([0:00](https://youtu.be/p3T3gEFK_KE?t=0)).
- PFOF was pioneered by Bernie Madoff, and the UK banned it in 2012 ([1:03](https://youtu.be/p3T3gEFK_KE?t=63)).
- Social networks make money "by selling user data to advertisers" ([4:11](https://youtu.be/p3T3gEFK_KE?t=251)).
- "Zero commission" is not free, because you pay through the price spread ([6:18](https://youtu.be/p3T3gEFK_KE?t=378)).

**Fact-check**
- ✅ The SEC settled in Dec 2020: **$65 million**. Robinhood hid PFOF, and its worse prices cost customers about **$34.1M**, even after the commissions they saved ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2020/12/17/robinhood-sec-charges-settlement-65-million-trading-app/); [ThinkAdvisor](https://thinkadvisor.com/2020/12/17/sec-fines-robinhood-65m-for-misleading-investors-best-execution-failures)). Boyle had guessed "over $10M", so the real fine was bigger.
- ⚠️ Madoff: his firm was a major *early promoter* of PFOF for listed shares, but not the only pioneer ([Quartz](https://qz.com/1894874/how-bernie-madoff-enabled-the-us-retail-trading-boom)).
- ⚠️ UK: the regulator (the FSA) issued 2012 guidance that *effectively* banned PFOF; it was not a new law ([CFA Institute](https://blogs.cfainstitute.org/marketintegrity/2016/08/17/payment-for-order-flow-uk-market-more-competitive-in-retail-trades-after-ban/)).
- ⚠️ "Selling user data": Facebook and Twitter mostly sell *targeting* (advertisers reach you without getting your data). It's a common simplification.

**Techniques:** calm expert authority, balance (he partly defends Robinhood), and an ironic old advert at the end. **For 6.10:** "free" as a frame that hides the real price.

## 4. Johnny Harris (propaganda, secret agencies): supports 6.8 and the movies topic

### 4a. "Why North Korea is so good at propaganda" (19 Apr 2023, 31:18, 3.8M) [watched: every minute, shortened blocks]
**What it claims**
- North Korea's official story is that Kim Jong Il was born on sacred Mt Paektu. Harris says it was really 1941 at a Soviet base, under a Russian name ([5:24](https://youtu.be/chAcK_3zBSY?t=324)).
- Kim had a library of 20,000 bootleg films with 250 staff ([6:28](https://youtu.be/chAcK_3zBSY?t=388)).
- In 1967 his father needed film to secure power; the son took over the studio ([7:30](https://youtu.be/chAcK_3zBSY?t=450)).
- Films preach self-reliance, praise suffering, and were shown in factories, farms and army units ([12:46](https://youtu.be/chAcK_3zBSY?t=766), [14:52](https://youtu.be/chAcK_3zBSY?t=892)).
- In 1978 he kidnapped actress Choi Eun-hee and director Shin Sang-ok, who made six films for him, including *Pulgasari* ([22:12](https://youtu.be/chAcK_3zBSY?t=1332)).

**Fact-check**
- ✅ Birth myth: the official account says Paektu, 1942; Soviet records say Vyatskoye, 1941. Historians dispute the exact place, and one historian (Tertitskiy) says the "born as Yuri" story is a misconception ([New World Encyclopedia](https://www.newworldencyclopedia.org/entry/Kim_Jong-il); [Wikipedia: Vyatskoye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vyatskoye,_Khabarovsk_Krai)). So it's ⚠️ on the name, ✅ on the myth.
- ✅ The kidnapping: Choi in January 1978, Shin six months later; they escaped in Vienna in 1986. "Six films" matches one source; counts vary ([Wikipedia: Shin Sang-ok](https://Www.Wikipedia.org/wiki/Sang-ok_Shin); [Dazed](https://www.dazeddigital.com/artsandculture/article/33047/1/the-north-korean-dictator-who-kidnapped-two-movie-stars)).
- ❓ 20,000 films and 250 staff: I could not confirm the numbers.

**Techniques:** a cinematic cold open (a reconstructed kidnapping), mood music, and mocking asides ("this guy sounds literally insane"). Mockery makes the enemy look absurd, which is itself an "enemy image" technique. The sponsor is a data-privacy service.

**What it claims**
- His own rule: a US official must be involved, with concrete evidence ([1:00](https://youtu.be/_wIOqHSsV9c?t=60)).

## 5. Movies and propaganda: the *Dhurandhar* debate, one film seen from four sides

**Background** [summary]: *Dhurandhar* (Dec 2025, director Aditya Dhar) follows a fictional Indian spy inside Karachi's Lyari gangs and is "inspired by real events". It became one of the highest-grossing Indian films ever (over ₹1,100 crore worldwide). Ladakh made it tax-free ([Bollywood Hungama](https://www.bollywoodhungama.com/news/bollywood/dhurandhar-declared-tax-free-ladakh-amid-successful-box-office-run/)). The sequel (Mar 2026) set off an open political fight: opposition parties called it "paid propaganda", the BJP defended it, and critics noted its use of archival footage of PM Modi ([NewKerala](https://www.newkerala.com/news/o/dhurandhar-triggers-political-storm-parties-spar-propaganda-terrorism-creative-380)). I found **no evidence that the government funded it**. The issues are framing, mixing fact with fiction, and political rewards.

### 5a. Dhruv Rathee, "Reality of Dhurandhar Film" (20 Dec 2025, 29:22, 25.5M) [watched: every minute, English audio track, shortened blocks]
**What it claims**
- **A mirror test:** he invents a film, "Bhavandar", in which a bearded PM ignores 11 intelligence warnings before Pulwama. Would BJP supporters accept that as "just fiction inspired by real events"? That, he says, is what *Dhurandhar* does to Congress and Manmohan Singh ([0:00–4:13](https://youtu.be/wWIJNCU8OOs?t=0)).
- The Indian government has always denied sending spies into Pakistan; the film makes such an agent a hero ([7:22](https://youtu.be/wWIJNCU8OOs?t=442)).
- The real gangster Rehman Dakait was killed in a police encounter in August 2009 by SP Chaudhry Aslam; the film adds a fictional Indian agent ([8:36](https://youtu.be/wWIJNCU8OOs?t=516)).
- A real 2010–11 case about British currency paper is twisted into a minister helping Pakistan print fake notes, for which there is "not a shred of evidence" ([10:50](https://youtu.be/wWIJNCU8OOs?t=650)).
- Every government had intelligence failures, including Kandahar under Vajpayee and Uri and Pulwama under Modi; failures are not treason ([12:59](https://youtu.be/wWIJNCU8OOs?t=779)).
- NSA Ajit Doval called an old clip of himself a deepfake ([20:35](https://youtu.be/wWIJNCU8OOs?t=1235)).
- The film's villains say only "Allahu Akbar", which works as a dog whistle against Muslims ([21:37](https://youtu.be/wWIJNCU8OOs?t=1297)).
- A comparison with Hitler's filmmaker Leni Riefenstahl ([6:20](https://youtu.be/wWIJNCU8OOs?t=380), [28:07](https://youtu.be/wWIJNCU8OOs?t=1687)).

**Fact-check**
- ✅ Pulwama warnings: *Frontline* (2021) reported at least 11 inputs between 2 Jan and 13 Feb 2019; officials say they were general ([The Wire](https://m.thewire.in/article/politics/pulwama-attack-happened-despite-reliable-intelligence-inputs-report); [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/pulwama-terror-attack-happened-despite-intelligence-inputs-frontline-report-2091398/)).
- ✅ De La Rue: in 2011 the minister told the Rajya Sabha the paper was defective and security clearance was denied ([Business Standard](https://business-standard.com/article/economy-policy/uk-based-co-denied-security-nod-to-supply-currency-paper-111122000179_1.html)). Nothing I found supports the film's version.
- ✅ Rehman Dakait was killed on 9 Aug 2009 in an encounter led by Chaudhry Aslam; his family called it fake ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rehman_Dakait); [News24](https://news24online.com/entertainment/who-was-sp-chaudhry-aslam-pakistans-most-controversial-police-officer-killed-gangster-rehman-dakait-played-by-sanjay-dutt-in-dhurandhar/683693/)).
- ✅ 26/11: the NSG arrived about 9–10 hours after the first shots; some teams had no night-vision gear ([Caravan](https://caravanmagazine.in/perspectives/force-alarm-failings-national-security-guard); [ORF](https://www.orfonline.org/expert-speak/chaos-counter-terrorism-india-45732)).
- ✅ Doval clip: Alt News and BOOM traced it to a 2014 Australia India Institute lecture and found it genuine ([BOOM](https://www.boomlive.in/fact-check/ajit-doval-deepfake-claim-isi-recruitment-video-fact-check-boomlive-30002); [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/nsa-ajit-doval-claims-old-video-of-him-saying-more-hindus-recruited-by-isi-than-muslims-is-deepfake-fact-check-shows-otherwise-3801667)).
- ❓ "Only Allahu Akbar, never Pakistan Zindabad": his reading of the film; I did not see the film.
- ⚠️ Riefenstahl: the facts about her are right, but the comparison is an association technique, not evidence.

**Techniques:** the mirror test is a genuinely good critical-thinking tool. He also uses heavy name-calling ("licking boots", "Godi ecosystem", "jokers", actors as "hired help"), a Nazi comparison, guesses at motives, and a sweeping claim that viewers who enjoy violence are "frustrated people". His sponsor slot advertises his own course. **Most of his checkable facts held up; his framing is as loaded as the film he criticises.**

### 5b. Mohak Mangal (Soch), "WTF is wrong with Dhurandhar? Hint: Nothing" (9 Dec 2025, 17:05, 1.7M) [watched: all]
**What it claims**
- The video is only about **violence**: four types (narrative, stylised, reflective, exploitative), with examples from the Mahabharata, *Saving Private Ryan*, *RRR*, *Visaranai* and *Marco* ([1:02–10:57](https://youtu.be/VLQhxgObuIk?t=62)).
- *Dhurandhar*'s violence is mostly narrative or stylised, so it is acceptable ([13:01](https://youtu.be/VLQhxgObuIk?t=781)).
- The film "has several issues, but violence is not one", and he never says what the issues are ([14:11](https://youtu.be/VLQhxgObuIk?t=851)).
- He criticised *Kabir Singh* and *Animal* because violence against women is relatable; he cites the NFHS, about 33% of women facing domestic violence ([15:15](https://youtu.be/VLQhxgObuIk?t=915)).
