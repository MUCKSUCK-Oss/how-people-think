## The most useful part for the paper: the channel itself as an example
The channel *uses* the tricks it criticises. That fits Section 1.1 (persuasion vs manipulation) and the round 1 and round 2 finding that **people can spot a trick and still be moved by it**.
- **Rage-bait and curiosity-gap titles:** "Buying GTA 6 MIGHT be for Retards" (335k views), "Does Joe Rogan Deserve To Live?". Shocking or insulting titles matched with "MIGHT" to keep the claim deniable.
- **Negativity sells, said while selling negativity:** in "Why Youtubers Love Wasting Your Time" he complains that every video is negative, while his own intro "talks shit about" another creator. He admits this himself as a "plot twist".
- **Fake-deadline upsells at the end of videos:**
  - "I am increasing the price in 30 days";
  - "There will be one day left for you to join for free until it becomes paid".
  - This is the scarcity/urgency trick (Cialdini; the FOMO section, 9.2), used in the same video that warns about scammers using urgency.
- **"The five people you hang around with" + "If I check your finances, I can tell what type of person you are"** are social-identity pressure used to sell a paid community.
