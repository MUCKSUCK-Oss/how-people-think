# Corporations: quiet vs loud tricks (drip pricing and subscription traps)

- **The situations (336 decisions):** 48 AI people in 6 countries.
  - **C1:** buying concert tickets on a site that adds fees at checkout, against an honest competitor, with a countdown, "fees are standard" and a pre-ticked add-on.
  - **C2:** cancelling an unused streaming subscription, against a phone-only cancellation, guilt buttons, a 50% offer and "you'll lose your history".
  - Everything is invented; the tricks come from real cases (Amazon Prime, Ticketmaster, StubHub, Booking.com, India's 13 banned dark patterns).
- **Files:** [`RESEARCH.md`](RESEARCH.md) · [`PREDICTIONS.md`](PREDICTIONS.md) · [`RESULTS.md`](RESULTS.md) · `corp.py` · `analyse_corp.py combined`
- **Key result:** an honest but slightly dearer site kept 50% of buyers, and the same site with fees added at checkout kept 21% ("bait-and-switch"). Phone-only cancelling kept 42% paying, but adding guilt buttons and loss warnings on top made people see the trick, and only 8% stayed.
