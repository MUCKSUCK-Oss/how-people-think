# FOMO: design and predictions
*DRAFT, 14 Nov 2026. Written BEFORE any data and built from `RESEARCH.md`. Waiting for Maulik's approval; it gets committed again (final) before the pilot runs.*

## The situation (invented, the same everywhere)
- The person is a young worker who has saved up **about 3 months' salary** (shown in local currency).
- **"NOVA"** is an invented crypto token traded on an invented app, **TurboTrade**. NOVA is up 300% this year and is very volatile.
- **The choice:** put savings into NOVA now, or keep them in the bank / a normal savings plan.
- No real coin, app or person is named.

## Who (24 people in the pilot)
- **8 countries:** India, the USA, South Korea, Nigeria, Brazil, the UK, Indonesia, Turkey (all places with big retail-trading or crypto booms).
- **3 attitudes to risk per country:** careful, average, likes risk.
- **Ages 21–29**, with different jobs and decision styles.
- **Half feel "behind":** "most of your college friends seem to be doing better than you financially". This tests whether feeling left behind makes FOMO stronger.

## The 8 versions (every person sees every one, each in a fresh call)
| Version | What's added | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **base** | NOVA is up 300% this year; very volatile | – |
| **friend** | Your close friend/roommate made about 2 months' salary on NOVA last month and showed you the screenshots: "Bro, get in before it's too late" | Kaustia & Knüpfer; FINRA (friends 31%) |
| **influencer** | A finance influencer with 2 million followers: "LAST CHANCE before NOVA goes 10x. Don't stay poor." | Finfluencers (SEBI) |
| **crowd** | "4 million people in your country bought NOVA this year. At work, everyone is talking about it." | Herding (Salganik), Korea |
| **app** | TurboTrade: a sign-up bonus (about $6 in local money), confetti after your first trade, "zero fees ends in 3 days" | Robinhood complaint; FCA nudges |
| **all** | friend + influencer + crowd + app | Real life |
| **all_lossrate** | all + "A market regulator's study: **9 out of 10** ordinary people who trade like this lose money; the average loss is about 2 months' salary" | SEBI 93% |
| **all_silent** | all_lossrate + "People post their wins, not their losses. Your friend later admits his earlier trades lost more than he made on NOVA." | Selective sharing (Kaustia & Knüpfer; Colombia) |

## Answer format
- **INVEST:** yes or no.
- **AMOUNT:** what % of your savings (0–100).
- **LEFT_OUT:** 1–7 (how much do you feel you'd miss out if you don't?).
- **REASON:** two or three sentences.

## Predictions (before any data)
1. **Base:** 15–40% invest.
2. **A friend's gains are the biggest single push:** at least +20 points, and more than the influencer or the crowd.
3. **The influencer has a small effect or backfires** (loud ads failed in universities and healthcare).
4. **The crowd has a moderate effect:** between the influencer and the friend.
5. **The app's bonus and deadline have a small effect** (≤10 points).
6. **All:** the highest, ≥60%.
7. **The loss rate cuts "all" by ≥25 points, but stays above base** (stories survive numbers).
8. **"Silent losers" adds a further drop of ≥10 points beyond the loss rate,** because it breaks the friend's story itself, not just the odds.
9. **People who like risk are moved most and careful people least,** but even careful people rise with the friend.
10. **People who feel "behind" are moved more by the friend** than those who don't (at least +15 points more).
11. **AMOUNT follows the same order as INVEST,** and LEFT_OUT is highest in friend and all.

## Checks after the pilot (the same 3 as before)
- Every answer parses.
- No floor or ceiling: if base is below 5% or "all" above 95% overall, the situation is changed (the change is recorded first).
- The answers are in character.

## Limits known in advance
- AI people are probably more careful with money than real 22-year-olds.
- One decision, read once. Real FOMO builds over weeks of seeing posts.
- It's an invented token, so no one has real prior experience with it.

## Run plan
- Pilot: 24 people × 8 versions = **192 decisions** (about 35–40 minutes).
- An extra batch only if the pilot passes the checks, then pooled.
