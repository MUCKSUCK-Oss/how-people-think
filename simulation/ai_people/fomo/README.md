# FOMO: friends, influencers, crowds and apps vs a risky token

- **The situation (384 decisions):** 48 AI young workers in 8 countries (India, the USA, South Korea, Nigeria, Brazil, the UK, Indonesia, Turkey) decide whether to put part of their savings into an invented, very volatile crypto token. Versions add a friend's gains, an influencer, a crowd number, an app bonus with confetti, all four, a "9 in 10 lose" warning, and the friend admitting their losses.
- **Files:** [`RESEARCH.md`](RESEARCH.md) · [`PREDICTIONS.md`](PREDICTIONS.md) · [`RESULTS.md`](RESULTS.md) · `fomo.py` · `analyse_fomo.py combined`
- **Key result:** the influencer and the app's tricks backfired (62% → 47% and 50%). A friend's gains pulled in careful people (12% → 31%). "9 in 10 lose" only made bets smaller, but the friend admitting their losses cut investing to 29%.
