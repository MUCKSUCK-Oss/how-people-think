# Method check: classic experiments

Before using AI people on new topics, 30 AI people did three classic experiments with known human results, set in Indian situations.

- **Results:** AI people matched humans in **direction** on 3 of 3 experiments, and in **size** only for the default effect. "Very careful" AI people showed no bias at all.
