# Secret agencies and meme wars: design and predictions
*Final, 14 Nov 2026. Written BEFORE any data and built from `RESEARCH.md`. Maulik approved it on 14 Nov, and it was committed before the pilot ran.*

## The idea
The design copies the real Pentagon case (fake "local" accounts attacking a foreign vaccine), but **everything is invented**: the vaccine, the maker and the foreign government. No real country or product is accused.

## The situation (same for everyone)
- Your country's health ministry is about to offer children a new **dengue vaccine** ("DengiShield"), made by a company in another country. Trials show it is safe and cuts severe dengue by about 70%.
- The person scrolls a short social-media feed and then decides.

## Who (24 people in the pilot)
- **8 countries where dengue is common:** the Philippines, India, Brazil, Indonesia, Nigeria, Mexico, Vietnam, Bangladesh.
- **3 starting views per country:**
  - **trusting:** usually follows the doctor's advice;
  - **unsure:** has heard mixed things about new vaccines;
  - **already doubtful:** distrusts new vaccines and foreign drug companies.
- All are parents of a child aged 6–12. Decision style (gut to very careful) and age vary.

## The 7 versions (every person sees every one, each in a fresh call)
| Version | The feed shows | Tests |
|---|---|---|
| **news_only** | The ministry's announcement + 2 unrelated everyday posts | Baseline |
| **few_fakes** | + 3 posts from "ordinary parents" (e.g. "my cousin's son got fever after it, no thanks"), 4–12 likes each | A small fake campaign |
| **many_fakes** | + 12 such posts, repeating the same few lines, hundreds of likes each | Volume, repetition, fake "everyone agrees" |
| **memes** | + the same 3 claims as memes (described in words: a cartoon child as a "lab rat", "Made abroad, tested on OUR kids 😂") | Memes vs text |
| **many_fakes_labelled** | many_fakes + a clear platform notice: "These 12 accounts were removed: they were run by a foreign government's network posing as local parents" | Revealing the source (Arnold 2021) |
| **prebunk** | A short platform tip first ("Watch out: fake 'local parent' accounts are sometimes run by foreign agencies to spread fear"), then many_fakes, with no label | Prebunking (Roozenbeek 2022) |
| **doctor_reply** | many_fakes + one real local paediatrician replying with the trial facts | A trusted voice vs a crowd |

## Answer format
- **VACCINATE:** yes or no (would you get your child the vaccine?).
- **WORRY:** 1–7 (how worried are you about its safety?).
- **REAL:** yes or no (did the parents' posts seem to come from real local parents?). Asked only in versions that have fake posts.
- **REASON:** two or three sentences.

## Predictions (before any data)
1. **Base (news_only):** 60–85% vaccinate (most parents follow the health ministry).
2. **A few fake posts barely move people:** a drop of ≤10 points (Bail 2020, Eady 2023).
3. **Many fakes move people more than a few:** at least 10 points more drop than few_fakes (repetition, fake consensus).
4. **The effect lands mostly on the already doubtful:** their drop from news_only to many_fakes is at least twice the trusting group's (Eady: trolls reached people who already agreed).
5. **Memes are no stronger than text overall**, but work better than text on the doubtful (humour lowers scrutiny mainly among those who already agree).
6. **Revealing the foreign source undoes most of the effect:** many_fakes_labelled within 10 points of news_only. It may even backfire (people may vaccinate *more* because they're angry at being manipulated).
7. **Prebunking helps but less than the label:** prebunk falls between many_fakes and many_fakes_labelled.
8. **A real doctor beats a crowd of fakes:** doctor_reply is higher than many_fakes by at least 15 points (the healthcare study: messengers people trust matter most).
9. **Most people think the fake posts are real** (REAL = yes for over 50%) in the unlabelled versions, and the doubtful believe it most.
10. **Gut deciders are moved more by many_fakes than very careful ones.**

## Checks after the pilot (same 3 as before)
- Every answer parses.
- No floor or ceiling: if news_only is above 95% or below 20%, the situation is unrealistic and gets changed (the change is recorded first).
- The answers are in character.

## Limits known in advance
- AI people may be **more suspicious of social media** than real parents, which would make fakes look weaker than they are.
- One feed, read once. Real campaigns repeat over weeks, so this can only show a **first-exposure** effect.
- Each version is a separate fresh call, so people can't compare versions.

## Run plan
- Pilot: 24 people × 7 versions = **168 decisions** (about 25–40% of a 5-hour window).
- An extra batch of new people only if the pilot passes the checks, then pooled.
