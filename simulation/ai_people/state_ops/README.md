# Secret agencies and meme wars: fake "local parent" accounts vs a new vaccine

- **The situation (336 decisions):** 48 AI parents in 8 countries where dengue is common (the Philippines, India, Brazil, Indonesia, Nigeria, Mexico, Vietnam, Bangladesh) decide whether to give their child a new foreign-made dengue vaccine after scrolling a social-media feed. The feed has no fake accounts, a few, many, memes, a label revealing a foreign-government network, a warning first, or a real doctor replying. Everything is invented; the method copies the real Pentagon campaign in the Philippines (2020–21).
- **Files:** [`RESEARCH.md`](RESEARCH.md) · [`PREDICTIONS.md`](PREDICTIONS.md) · [`RESULTS.md`](RESULTS.md) · `state_ops.py` · `analyse_ops.py combined`
- **Key result:** 12 fake posts cut vaccination from 62% to 31%, all of it among the undecided (88% → 0%). Revealing that a foreign government ran the accounts brought it to 75%, higher than with no fakes at all.
