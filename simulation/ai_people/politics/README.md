# World politics: same policy, different party label

- **The situation (336 decisions):** 48 AI people in 8 countries rate the same invented policy when it is proposed by economists, by their own party or by the rival party, in a plain or a rally style, and with a fact-check or a U-turn reminder.
- **Files:** [`RESEARCH.md`](RESEARCH.md) · [`PREDICTIONS.md`](PREDICTIONS.md) · [`RESULTS.md`](RESULTS.md) · `politics.py` · `analyse_pol.py combined`
- **Key result:** the same words scored 5.5 of 7 from a supporter's own party and 2.9 from the rival, in all 8 countries. The effect came mostly from disliking the rival, and fact-checks barely changed it.
