## Psychology / how rulers & powerful play with minds (WTF, new)
- **Omar Sultan Al Olama (UAE minister) 26:47-32:56** https://youtu.be/9gX2-RvNhf8?t=1607 : Nikhil asks Machiavelli's fear-vs-love question. Al Olama: manipulators "don't survive for long"; "you can trick many people for a short time or a few for a long time, but not many for a long time" (the Lincoln-attributed line; attribution is disputed); social media exposes any act; Commodus couldn't sustain his act. THEN says the Al Maktoum family (rulers of Dubai since 1833) "not a single one puts on an act... what you see on social media is what he is". OH-MOMENT: a serving minister telling a mass audience that his own ruler is authentic IS image management; can't be checked from outside.
